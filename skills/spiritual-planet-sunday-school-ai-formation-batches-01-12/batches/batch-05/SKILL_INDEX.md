# Batch 05 Skill Index

总入口：`$spiritual-planet-parent-formation-orchestrator`

| Skill | 用途 |
|---|---|
| `$spiritual-planet-parent-formation-orchestrator` | Parent Formation Orchestrator：Parents receive a grace-first, non-surveillant path to change their own attention, anxiety, authority, repair and example before attempting to govern children. |
| `$christian-parent-digital-mirror` | Parent Digital and Spiritual Mirror：Parents can compare stated values with their own observable habits without blaming children or receiving a parental-fitness judgment. |
| `$christian-parent-attention-availability` | Parent Attention Availability：Parents can increase reliable relational availability through small observable actions rather than monitoring child attention. |
| `$christian-parent-anxiety-transmission` | Parent Anxiety Transmission Reflection：Parents can own and regulate their fear without making children responsible for adult emotional stability. |
| `$christian-family-success-idol-discernment` | Family Success and Status Discernment：Families can reorder success under faithfulness, honesty, love, worship and rest without an automated idolatry verdict. |
| `$christian-parent-child-repair` | Parent–Child Repair：Parents can repair real harm without excuses, role reversal, forced forgiveness or storing the child’s private disclosure. |
| `$christian-parent-authority-grace` | Parent Authority, Grace and Consequences：Family authority becomes transparent, bounded and restorative rather than arbitrary, shaming or spiritually coercive. |
| `$christian-co-parent-governance` | Co-Parent Governance：Adult caregivers can coordinate rules without using children as messengers, allies or surveillance targets. |
| `$christian-child-feedback-invitation` | Child Feedback Invitation：Children can describe ordinary experience safely without being tested for belief, forced to disclose secrets or exposed to retaliation. |
| `$christian-parent-example-plan` | 30-Day Parent Example Plan：Parents receive a small, sustainable plan that measures their chosen actions rather than children’s outcomes. |
| `$christian-parent-safety-boundary` | Parent and Child Safety Boundary：The system stops ordinary formation when protection is needed and hands off minimal information to authorized humans. |
| `$parent-formation-course-integrator` | Parent Formation Course Integrator：The parent module is coherent, accessible and safely connected to household roles without exposing child records or bypassing review. |
