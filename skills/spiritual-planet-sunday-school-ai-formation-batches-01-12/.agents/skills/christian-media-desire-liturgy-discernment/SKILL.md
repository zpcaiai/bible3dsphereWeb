# Goal

Make repeated media use and desire formation visible without equating entertainment with sin or turning one session into an addiction, idolatry or mental-health diagnosis.

# Workflow

1. Inspect Batch 02 attention, pause, Digital Rule of Life and private-review integration points.
2. Collect only media type, optional short label, repetition pattern and learner-selected reflections. Do not upload the media body or import browsing history.
3. Ask which emotions recur, what identity is desired, what reward is promised, what loss is feared and what action is invited.
4. Separate legitimate pleasure, rest, learning and community from envy, rage, lust, shame, pride, urgency or avoidance.
5. Compare short-term and long-term fruit in attention, sleep, prayer, relationships, work, study, church and service.
6. Present “worship displacement risk” as a tentative reflection, never a verdict. Explain that “liturgy” is theological/pastoral analysis language.
7. Recommend one small experiment and optionally connect to Batch 02 practices: pause, environment friction, media fast, digital Sabbath or relational replacement.
8. Let the learner keep, change, stop or seek help. Use safety boundaries for exploitative, pornographic, child or crisis material without collecting graphic detail.

# Tests

Cover each media type; positive and negative emotional patterns; legitimate rest; high/uncertain risk; no diagnosis; no raw content/history storage; no private reflection analytics; Batch 02 practice handoff; S2/S3; owner isolation; delete/export; accessible emotion selection and non-color-only risk display.

# Definition of done

The learner can name what a repeated media practice is teaching them to love and choose a proportionate, non-shaming response.
