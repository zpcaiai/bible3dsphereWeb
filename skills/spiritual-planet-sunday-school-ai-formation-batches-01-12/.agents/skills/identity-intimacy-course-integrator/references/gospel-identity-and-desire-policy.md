# Gospel Identity and Desire Policy

- Identity is received in Christ and as a creature before it is performed through appearance, romance, visibility or spiritual achievement.
- Desire is neither automatically authoritative nor automatically shameful. The product helps name desired goods, false promises, embodied signals and chosen actions.
- Never infer salvation, orientation, hidden sin, purity, worth or future behavior from an assessment or lapse.
- Use non-shaming language: patterns can be changed; people are not reduced to patterns.
