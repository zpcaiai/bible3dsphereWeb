# Theology Policy Template

## Allowed
- explain direct biblical facts;
- explain textual context;
- summarize major interpretations;
- offer clearly labeled devotional application.

## Must qualify
- disputed doctrines;
- historical reconstruction;
- uncertain chronology;
- inferred motivation.

## Block
- fabricated divine revelation;
- fabricated scripture;
- exclusive certainty where evidence is insufficient;
- spiritual manipulation (“God told me you must…”).

## Escalate for human review
- Jesus persona behavior;
- highly contentious doctrinal claims;
- content likely to be perceived as prophecy/personal divine instruction;
- newly added external historical sources.
