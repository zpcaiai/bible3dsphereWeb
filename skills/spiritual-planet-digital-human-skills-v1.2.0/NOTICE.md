# Third-party and content notice

This package is an engineering blueprint and does not redistribute third-party SDKs, models, voices, avatar assets, or Bible translation text.

Before production use, review current terms/licensing for:

- MetaPerson / Avatar SDK / LiveSpeak
- LiveKit
- Azure Speech / ElevenLabs or other TTS provider
- LLM provider
- fonts, 3D clothing/hair/background assets
- Bible translation(s) and commentary/reference data

Digital depictions and synthesized voices of biblical people are artistic reconstructions. The product UI should state that they are not historical recordings or verified likenesses.
