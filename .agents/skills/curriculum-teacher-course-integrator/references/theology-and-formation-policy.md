# Theology and Formation Policy — Batch 09

## Authority layering

Every spiritual content item preserves one of:

- `SCRIPTURE_EXPLICIT`
- `THEOLOGICAL_INFERENCE`
- `PASTORAL_WISDOM`
- `PRODUCT_DEFAULT`

A product rule, age default, device boundary, curriculum sequence, assessment question or recovery practice must not be presented as a direct divine command unless the reviewed content actually supports that classification.

## Gospel order

Identity and grace precede performance. Practices are means of formation and wise structure; they do not earn salvation, prove regeneration or establish a believer's comparative worth.

## Embodiment and community

The body is created and worthy of care, not an enemy to punish. Formation includes embodied habits, truthful relationships, church life, responsibility and repair. Technology may assist but may not replace prayer, repentance, covenant, pastoral office, sacraments, protection duties or embodied care.

## Human review

Generated theological, sexual-formation, child/youth, pastoral or curriculum content remains in a review state until the correct reviewers approve it. The product must show the review/version state internally and support rollback.
