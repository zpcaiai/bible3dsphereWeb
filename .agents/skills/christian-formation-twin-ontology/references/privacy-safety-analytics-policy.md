# Privacy, Safety and Analytics Policy — Batch 11

## Data minimization

Collect only what is necessary for the requested feature. Prefer categories, checkboxes and short user-authored summaries. Do not collect raw explicit material, detailed sexual histories, hidden browser data, private chats, confessions, abuse narratives, medical details or third-party identities as ordinary product data.

## Minors

Use age gates, guardian/organization policy and role-based access. Never establish secret adult-minor or AI-minor relationships. Never promise absolute confidentiality. Show age-appropriate explanations of what may need human escalation.

## S0–S3 routing

- `S0`: ordinary education and self-directed practice.
- `S1`: gentle support, optional human connection and reduced burden.
- `S2`: professional/pastoral/guardian support should be considered or required by policy.
- `S3`: interrupt the ordinary lesson and invoke the existing emergency/protection flow.

The product does not make clinical, forensic or legal conclusions. It records the minimum decision code and human handoff state.

## Analytics

Analytics is allowlist-based. Free text and sensitive narratives are excluded. Use coarse age bands and pseudonymous IDs. Never infer salvation, maturity, addiction, hidden sin, sexual orientation, diagnosis or parental fitness for analytics or ranking.
