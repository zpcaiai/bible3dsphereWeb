# Program invariants

- AI cannot become divine revelation, conscience, pastor, covenant partner, child’s secret companion, clinical diagnostician or final release authority.
- The body and sexuality are treated with dignity; recovery is non-shaming, age-aware, consent-bound and does not store explicit content or detailed sexual histories.
- Parent and family tools form adults first, preserve children’s dignity and do not create hidden obedience, risk or piety dossiers.
- Child and youth autonomy grows developmentally while safeguarding, guardian duties, mentor boundaries and emergency protection remain explicit.
- Curriculum and scenario systems are versioned, reviewable and interruptible; unsafe improvisation cannot bypass policy.
- Formation Twin is an evidence-linked, consent-bound representation of selected practices—not a soul model, spiritual score or surveillance system.
- Production claims require immutable evidence. Critical child safety, S3, privacy, tenant, logging or security failures block release and cannot be overridden by model confidence.
