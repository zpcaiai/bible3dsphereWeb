# Goal

Create small spaces between desire and action so the learner can choose what is truthful, loving and faithful rather than automatically obeying an impulse.

# Practice families

Implement configurable versions of:

- two-to-ten-minute urge delay;
- intention before unlock;
- content queue instead of immediate watching;
- nonessential purchase waiting period;
- short periods of no-input boredom;
- single-task focus;
- moving a device away from the trigger context;
- choosing a real-world replacement action.

# Workflow

1. Implement or validate practice catalog entries.
2. Provide gentle, standard and stretch variants.
3. Every practice must define a minimum viable action.
4. Treat a lapse as information about trigger and environment, not proof of character failure.
5. Let the learner stop or reduce a practice.
6. Connect intense or unsafe patterns to the pause and safety boundaries.
7. Record only categorical barriers and observed fruit.
8. Do not create willpower rankings or competitive challenges.

# Guardrails

- No food restriction unless routed through the body-rhythm fasting gate.
- No sleep restriction.
- No exposure to harmful content as “training.”
- No humiliation or public accountability without explicit consent.
- Do not tell someone to remain in danger or neglect caregiving duties to complete a practice.

# Tests

Cover triggered and scheduled practices, minimum action, reduction, replacement, lapse copy, categorical check-in, privacy, no streak punishment, no unsafe deprivation, plan-engine integration and accessibility.

# Definition of done

The learner can practice waiting in small, realistic ways and translate that space into a faithful next action.
